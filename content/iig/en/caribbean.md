### Dossier: Karibisches Resonanzexperiment

Summary by the IRG data analysis department.

### Project goals

* to test whether an anomalous resonance can be created outside the CAZ;
* to reproduce the conditions under which anomalous energy forms;
* to study the global connectivity of the Earth's information field.

### Why the Caribbean

High geomagnetic activity, a stable climate and distance from the major powers.

### Outcome

The experiment ended in catastrophe. Outside the CAZ, short-lived gravitational disturbances, satellite communication failures, mental disorders among the staff and the disappearance of equipment were recorded. The project was shut down and the data classified. After the experiment the boundary of the CAZ's anomalous environment moved several kilometres outward.

### The IRG's position

The unofficial conclusion of the scientific community: the CAZ is not a local phenomenon but a node in a global system. One of the reasons the IRG was founded was to make sure such experiments are never again carried out without international oversight.
