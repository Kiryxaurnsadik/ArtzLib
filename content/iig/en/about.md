### IRG

The **International Research Group** is a joint scientific mission of specialists from several countries, working in the CAZ under an international mandate. The group's reports are kept in German and Russian.

### Tasks

* independent study of the phenomena of the CAZ;
* investigation into the consequences of the Caribbean experiment (Karibisches Resonanzexperiment);
* monitoring the global connectivity of the Earth's information field;
* drawing up international safety protocols for work in the Zone.

### Principles

* Data belong to the whole scientific community. Reports are published for all parties to the mandate.
* The group does not arm itself beyond what is needed for self-defence.
* Decisions are taken by the group council, not by a single leader.

### Motto

*"Scientia sine finibus": science without borders.*
