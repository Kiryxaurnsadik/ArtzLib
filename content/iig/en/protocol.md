### IRG data handling protocol

1. Every report is kept in two copies: the original and a translation (DE / RU).
2. A report is signed by its author and by the head of the department.
3. Artifact samples are transported only in shielded containers.
4. Information about psi installations and relays is classified as "restricted".
5. If a terminal is compromised, its data are wiped remotely on the council's order.

### Terminal statuses

* **NORMAL**: regular operation.
* **GESPERRT**: access locked by the group council.
* **GELÖSCHT**: data wiped according to protocol.
