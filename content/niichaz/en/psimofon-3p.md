### Background to the upgrade
The PZ-2 upgrade revealed the key problem of all previous generations:

* protection was based on **generating an antiphase field**, which required a lot of energy.

The discovery of alloys with pronounced **psi-absorbing properties of germanium (Ge-32)** made it possible to move to a new concept:

* **not countering psi radiation, but absorbing and dispersing it.**

![](assets/docs/1059cd3750.webp)

This became a turning point in the development of personal protective equipment.

### General concept of the "Diadem-32"
The PZ-3P is a hybrid neuro-absorbing headband that forms a local protective circuit around the brain. Unlike previous generations:

* the protective field is no longer created by generators alone.
* a significant part of the protection is provided by the materials of the design itself.

As a result, the device has become:

* compact.
* energy-efficient.
* suitable for covert wear.

### The move to psi-absorbing alloys

The design is based on a multilayer composite whose layers include:

1. **A germanium-containing psi-absorbing alloy**
   * absorbs psi waves.
   * converts them into a thermal microsignal.
   * prevents biofield resonance.
2. **A neuro-induction matrix**
   * stabilises the biofield.
   * forms a local shielding circuit.
3. **A conductive network of point stimulation**
   * acts on key centres of thought.
   * reduces the brain's susceptibility to external pressure.

*(see Illustration No. 3)*

![](assets/docs/6cb84ee368.webp)

### The new active psi protection principle

The PZ-3P works according to the following three-stage scheme.

### I. Absorption
The germanium alloy takes the main flow of psi radiation.

### II. Dispersion
The absorbed energy is dispersed as harmless thermal noise.

### III. Neurostabilisation
Point neuro-induction nodes:

* synchronise brain rhythms;
* block external impulses from being imposed;
* stabilise thought processes.

Since the main protection is now passive:

* the generators work only in stabilisation mode;
* power consumption is reduced many times over;
* autonomous operating time is longer;
* there is practically no heat signature.

The PZ-3P became the first psi device suitable for **long-term continuous wear**. Its main visible difference, however, is how compact it is:

* it is worn as a neuro-headband;
* it fits under helmets and hard hats;
* it does not interfere with other equipment;
* it does not give the wearer away.

This removes the main drawback of the PZ-1 and PZ-2, as the PZ-3P provides:

### Full protection against:

* background psi radiation.
* residual effects of anomalies.
* prolonged psi pressure of the environment.

### High protection against:

* directed psi exposure of medium and elevated power.

### Partial protection against:

* extreme psi events.
* emissions.
* the influence of highly organised psi entities.

### Conclusion
The "Diadem-32" marked the transition from bulky laboratory devices to practical everyday protection for researchers and fighters in the CAZ. The third generation has firmly established the possibility of:

* covert wear,
* long autonomous operation,
* mass introduction into field units.

The PZ-3P is regarded as the basic standard of individual psi protection for the new era.
