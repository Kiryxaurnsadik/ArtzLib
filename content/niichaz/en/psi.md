In research on the Chernobyl Anomalous Zone, psi radiation is understood as a special type of wave exposure (psychotropic, psionic) that has a direct effect on the neurophysiological processes of living organisms, bypassing the usual sensory channels of perception.

The key feature of this phenomenon is that its primary target is not the body but the brain. The exposure is aimed at destroying or reprogramming higher cognitive functions, including will, memory, perception and the ability to make one's own decisions.

Psi radiation is one of the most dangerous factors of the Zone, since it can change a person's behaviour and personality without direct physical contact.

### The biofield theory
One of the main working hypotheses is the existence of a so-called biofield: a subtle energy envelope of the organism formed by the combined work of:

* the nervous system
* the endocrine system
* the brain's own electromagnetic activity

Supposed functions of the biofield:

* shielding against external mental influences
* stabilising cognitive processes
* protecting individual will and behavioural autonomy

Damage to the integrity of the biofield leads to the symptoms observed in victims of psi exposure:

* apathy and depression
* submissiveness and loss of initiative
* cognitive degradation
* psychosomatic disorders

At present the existence of the biofield is regarded as a theory that needs further confirmation.

### Physical nature of psi radiation
Current data allow psi radiation to be regarded as a vector wave field partly overlapping the ranges of:

* high-frequency sound (ultrasound),
* infrasound,
* weak electromagnetic oscillations,
* an unidentified information component (code).

It has been confirmed experimentally that the frequency range from 10,000 to 12,000 Hz distorts the human biofield, creating local ruptures in it. As the frequency rises further, the effect grows non-linearly.

The key feature of psi radiation is its built-in information signal, which can act directly on the synaptic connections of the brain and serve as both a command and a program.

### Neuropsychological exposure and the suggestion effect
Experiments with NLP coding using a BESM-6 computer established that directed wave exposure can create false sensory and vestibular sensations in a subject that completely ignore the real position of the body.

Of fundamental importance:

* the brain does not distinguish psi code from real sensory experience.
* the exposure does not require conscious perception.
* the effect occurs even when nothing is heard.

This explains the high effectiveness of psi exposure in combat and anomalous conditions.

### Source of directed psi radiation
The mutated creature known as the "Controller" is the most dangerous biological source of psi radiation in the CAZ. The Controller generates a narrowly directed psi beam, whose activation becomes much stronger on eye contact. The Controller's eyes serve to:

* focus the field,
* synchronise the psi code with the target.

Even brief visual contact leads to a sharp drop in the stability of the human biofield.
Even without direct contact, the following are recorded in areas where a Controller is present:

* auditory hallucinations (whispers, voices, crackling),
* a feeling of pressure in the head,
* brief memory lapses.

These phenomena are explained by background psi emission distorting the processing of auditory signals in the cerebral cortex.

### How psi radiation causes damage
The sequence of exposure is as follows:

1. Background psi radiation causes a feeling of pressure in the head or weakens the biofield.
2. A directed pulse burns out neural circuits completely or starts the process of rupturing the biofield.
3. The psi code is introduced into neural circuits no longer protected by the biofield.
4. The autonomy of the person's brain behaviour is disrupted.
5. In severe cases this can mean complete submission or mental degradation, and with directed radiation, brain death.

### Potential developments against psi radiation, based on theory
The priority areas are considered to be:

* Decoding the psi code and the signal of each unique psi radiation,
* Studying the interaction of the psi field with gravitational and electromagnetic anomalies (a potential solution),
* Developing protective clothing elements with layers of ferromagnetic and composite materials able to create a chaotic signal that destroys the structure of the psi code.
* Short-term equipment or preparations to protect the biofield from the Controller's directed psi radiation.

### Conclusion

Psi radiation is one of the most dangerous and least studied factors of the Chernobyl Anomalous Zone. Its effectiveness is based not on destroying the body but on rewriting perception and will. In this context the Controller is not an ordinary predator of the CAZ but a living example of controllable, directed psi radiation.
