### Overview

Duty is a paramilitary faction with strict discipline and a charter. Its goal is to protect the outside world from the Zone, contain the spread of anomalies and exterminate mutants. Duty's main ideological opponent remains Freedom.

### Attitude to science

Duty supports research groups whose work is aimed at studying threats and neutralising them. Its fighters readily hand mutant parts and samples over to scientists but oppose the commercial use of artifacts.

### Internal divisions

According to the "Diskette" (AI-O/1), a separate formation within Duty, the "Reapers Wing", stands apart, its goals and methods differing from those of the main structure. A fighter with the call sign "Chess Player" is mentioned.
