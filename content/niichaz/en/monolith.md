### Overview

Monolith is a closed paramilitary community whose members worship the Monolith crystal, which supposedly grants wishes and is said to lie near the fourth reactor unit of the ChNPP. The community's fighters do not negotiate, attack any outsiders and hold the approaches to the centre of the Zone: Pripyat, the ChNPP and the Radar area.

### The nature of their obedience

According to stalkers and indirect conclusions from our research, Monolith adherents are under constant psi influence. The signal is maintained by a network of relays (see the report "Improvised psi relay") and is presumably linked to a group of scientists known as "O-Consciousness". Loss of the signal leads to disorientation, memory gaps and, in some cases, to the fighter slipping out of control.

### Observed signs

* uniform equipment without insignia, suits of the Zircon and Corundum series;
* prayer formulas on the radio and during combat contact;
* abnormally high resistance to radiation combined with vulnerability to psi fields;
* no fear of emissions, although they still take cover.

### Link to Noon

After the control installations were destroyed, some former adherents united in the Noon community. It is a separate structure and is not the same as Monolith (see the report "The Noon community").
