Psi radiation research has established that the existing stationary means of suppressing psi radiation (in particular the ANP "Khaos-M" system based on the BESM-6 computer) are highly effective but unsuitable for individual use in the field. At the same time, the data accumulated on injuries among Varta fighters, stalkers and research staff showed that the greatest damage is done to the human brain and biofield, while the rest of the body is a secondary target.

This led to the concept of a local psi screen, designed to protect the central nervous system without trying to shield the whole body.

### Concept and operating principle
The first-generation psi screen is a deeply modified electrode cap whose operating principle is borrowed and scaled from the architecture of ANP "Khaos-M", but adapted to a portable format.

The key idea is not so-called jamming of psi radiation but the creation of a chaotic neurofield screen around the wearer's head, similar in function to a Faraday cage but acting across an extended range:

* electromagnetic,
* acoustic (infrasound and ultrasound),
* informational psi-wave.

The screen forms a zone of unstable field around the head, inside which the psi code loses coherence, cannot embed itself in synaptic circuits and cannot synchronise with the wearer's biofield.

### Design and elements

### Electrode dome
A multipoint system of spherical electrodes placed over the whole surface of the cap. The electrodes are connected by flexible shielded cables, forming a dynamic network with a constantly changing field configuration.
### Chaotic signal generator
A compact unit that creates pseudo-random multi-frequency pulses modelled on the noise profile of the BESM-6, but in a greatly simplified and power-hungry form.
### Control and diagnostic module
A side control unit that allows you to:

* monitor biofield parameters (indicator),
* register the degree of psi load and switch modes,
* connect scientific equipment for monitoring and data recording.

### Mounting system
An adjustable strap system designed for long wear, though it does not hide the bulk of the design.

### Effectiveness and limitations
The first-generation psi screen effectively protects the wearer's brain from indirect and background psi radiation, including:

* *Psi anomalies,*
* *Residual fields,*
* *Weak psi emissions from mutants,*
* *Perceptual distortions and auditory hallucinations.*

However, theoretical and experimental analysis has revealed fundamental limitations:

* *There is no protection against a Controller (because of its narrowly directed psi beam and visual synchronisation).*
* *There is no protection against emissions, since their psi structure is large-scale and cannot be synchronised.*
* *The screen does not restore a damaged biofield; it only temporarily prevents further damage.*

### Drawbacks of the device
Significant drawbacks include:

* Large size and weight, which limit mobility.
* High power consumption, requiring frequent recharging or an external power source.
* Heat load during prolonged operation.
* It cannot be used covertly.

These drawbacks are considered to be expected for a prototype and are seen as the price of a proven concept.

### Scientific and medical value
Despite being a field device, the PZ-1 proved exceptionally valuable as a scientific and rehabilitation instrument. Thanks to its broad compatibility with laboratory equipment, the screen can be used for:

* Diagnosing psi injuries,
* Monitoring biofield recovery,
* Studying neurofield distortions,
* Rehabilitating psi radiation victims under controlled conditions.

In effect, the PZ-1 became the first instrument that made it possible to observe psi effects on the brain in real time without exposing the subject to mortal risk.

### Conclusion
The first-generation psi screen is an experimental, heavy and power-hungry, but conceptually correct step towards individual protection against psi radiation. It confirmed that local neurofield shielding is possible and lays the foundation for further development of compact, wearable and more specialised psi protection systems.
