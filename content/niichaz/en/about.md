### NIIChAZ

The **Research Institute of the Chernobyl Anomalous Zone** is a state scientific body carrying out the systematic study of the anomalous fields, artifacts, fauna and psi radiation of the CAZ. The institute operates through a network of mobile bunker units (MBU), guarded by Varta detachments.

### Facilities

* **MBU Yantar**: a research bunker by Lake Yantar. Department of technical and medical development. Senior researcher: Associate Professor Burnstein K. F.
* **MBU Ikar**: a theoretical research group covering the phenomena of the CAZ, anomaly classification, fauna, psi radiation and emissions.

### Historical note

The first permanent scientific expeditions appeared in the Zone soon after the catastrophe of 2006. For a long time these were scattered groups and mobile laboratories, each with its own superiors and its own archives. NIIChAZ brought these lines of work under a single leadership and continued the work under Varta's protection.

### Areas of work

* development of psi protection (the 1P, 2P and 3P psi screen line);
* equipment for finding and storing artifacts (Medved, KDA-7, SKA-1);
* field medicine (PKhN-2, the Psi blocker);
* fundamental research into the phenomena of the CAZ.

### Motto

*"Wissen schützt": knowledge protects.*
