### How the preparation was obtained

I was studying the phenomenon of psi radiation. While I was stationed at the MBU talking with people of the stalker culture, a man from the Noon community came to us; members of this community used to be a dangerous group in the Zone known as Monolith. They do not remember now who they were, and they feel guilt for the cruelty they committed. One of them, call sign Inquisitor, introduced himself as a hermit who moves on his own, apart from everyone else in his community. He told me a little about himself, but he handed over these pills and said they might be more useful to us than to him. During an anomalous storm he felt unwell, which means his biofield had been torn by prolonged psi exposure; he said that any psi radiation is more painful for them than for anyone else in the CAZ. After a few minutes of our conversation he set off to the northern swamps with Professor Zernov and a Varta detachment; I have not seen him since and nothing has been heard of him.

### Rationale for the development

Field and laboratory studies have established that psi exposure in the Chernobyl Anomalous Zone has a primary destructive effect on the human biofield, and with prolonged exposure causes critical disturbances of the blood supply to the brain. This leads to symptoms similar to dyscirculatory encephalopathy: headaches, disorientation, aggression, loss of cognitive functions and, in extreme cases, intracerebral haemorrhage and death.

### How the psi blocker works

The psi blocker is a combined-action preparation aimed at two key protective mechanisms:

1. *Normalising and boosting cerebral circulation*
   The preparation increases the oxygen saturation of the blood, compensating for the disrupted delivery of energy to neurons in a psi field.
2. *Temporarily strengthening the human biofield*
   By acting on metabolic processes and blood flow speed, it creates a short-term densification of the biofield, raising resistance to vector psi radiation.

The main active component is humic acid in a modified state, mixed with an oxygen-saturated substance. A similar structure was found in the blood of a Controller, where it provides an abnormally high blood circulation speed (over 2000 mm/s), which points to its direct link with resistance to psi exposure.

### Use and limitations
The recommended single dose must strictly not exceed 0.1 mg.
Exceeding the dose leads to a sharp rise in intra-arterial pressure and can cause the opposite effect, namely a stronger negative effect on the brain and putting the person into an emotionless state. At present the preparation:

* is **NOT** a treatment.
* does **NOT** remove the consequences of psi damage.
* must be used **EXCLUSIVELY** as a temporary, non-permanent measure of protection against psi exposure.

The duration of the protective effect is limited and depends on the intensity of the psi field and the individual characteristics of the body. It has been established, however, that the effect lasts at most 5 minutes.

### Conclusion
The psi blocker is possibly the first systematic medical agent aimed not at suppressing symptoms but at temporarily stabilising the human biofield under psi exposure. In the conditions of the CAZ the preparation does not guarantee safety, but it significantly increases the chances of survival, especially in sudden contact with possible psi anomalies (although none have been observed) and with creatures such as the Controller.
