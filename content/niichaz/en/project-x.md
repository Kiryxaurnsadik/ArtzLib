### Abstract (Zusammenfassung)
Project "X" was a network of dispersed underground laboratories on the territory of the Chernobyl Anomalous Zone, set up for research in the fields of:

* Psi radiation
* Anomale Energie
* Mind control
* Interaction with the global information field

Fragments of documentation indicate that the final goal was to create a system known under the working name "Noosphere". Most of the original materials have been lost or destroyed. Below is a reconstruction based on the available data.

### General structure of Project "X"

Analysis of archive records shows that Project "X" was not a single laboratory. It was a verteilte Forschungsstruktur (distributed research structure). Each facility had its own index and specialisation:

* Neuropsychology

* Psi constructs

* Bioengineering

* Control of collective consciousness

The laboratories were located deep underground and were autonomous. The likely reason for this architecture was the need to hide the scale of the work and to minimise the consequences of possible accidents.

The laboratories themselves may have been built in USSR times and may even have drawn power from the ChNPP before and after its accident. Most of the laboratories found, however, had been abandoned, possibly because of military operations or failed bioengineering experiments.

### Main goal of the project

Surviving fragments of technical reports from various laboratories show that the project sought to create a system for influencing the collective thinking of humanity through the planet's information and energy field, the Noosphere. Frequent headings and terms in the documents were:

* Globales Information Field (Noosphere).

* Neuro-Resonanz.

* Psi-Synchronisation.

The researchers regarded human consciousness as an element of a single network. It was assumed that with a strong enough signal it would be possible to:

* Transmit commands directly to the brain.

* Suppress individual will.

* Control the behaviour of large groups of people.
