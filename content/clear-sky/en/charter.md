### Charter of the Clear Sky faction

1. **Science before profit.** Recovered artifacts are handed over to the base laboratory and are not sold to outside traders.
2. **Neutrality.** The faction does not interfere in the wars between Duty and Freedom. Fire is opened only in response.
3. **Emission protocol.** At the first signs of an emission (birds flying south en masse, a red sky, pressure in the temples) all fighters must reach cover within two minutes.
4. **Communications.** Every sortie is logged in the radio room journal. Radio silence lasting more than six hours means a search party.
5. **The Swamps are our home.** Watchtowers, guides and boats are common property.
6. **Renegades.** Gangs in the Swamps are disarmed whenever possible, not destroyed.

### Ranks

* **Rookie**: trains under the guides.
* **Fighter**: allowed on sorties beyond the Swamps.
* **Researcher**: keeps their own observation log.
* **Senior**: leads sorties and watchtowers.
