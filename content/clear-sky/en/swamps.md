### Report: the Swamps

### Terrain

The Swamps are a vast marshy lowland in the west of the Zone: reeds, channels, old villages, water towers and a church. Visibility is poor, especially in fog. Moving around without a guide is dangerous.

### Anomalous activity

* clusters of "electros" along the old power lines;
* chemical anomalous fields (CAF-2) in stagnant water;
* rare spatial anomalies near the flooded farms.

### Fauna

Fleshes, boars and blind dogs. Bloodsuckers have been seen at night: do not linger in the reeds after sunset.

### Threats

Renegades, bandit groups and looters. There are occasional attempts to seize the watchtowers.

### Recommendations

Use proven trails, do not wade into water deeper than your knees, and stay in contact with the base.
