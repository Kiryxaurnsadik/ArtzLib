### Clear Sky

**Clear Sky** is an independent scientific and military faction whose goal is to understand the nature of the Zone and preserve its balance. Its members do not chase money and try to stay out of territorial wars. The main base is in the Swamps, in the western part of the Zone.

### Historical note

According to the base archives, the faction's former members believed that emissions grow stronger in response to people's attempts to break through to the centre of the Zone. An attempt to stop such a breakthrough ended in heavy losses, and the faction almost ceased to exist. What remained of that base were empty watchtowers, flooded dugouts and logbooks that we are still sorting through.

### Revival

Our cell is made up of surviving researchers and fighters who returned to the Swamps. We have restored the watchtowers, rebuilt the laboratory and carry on with the main work: observing emissions and searching for patterns.

### Principles

* The Zone is a living system, not a resource.
* We do not shoot first. We do not sell data.
* Every emission is recorded. Every record is kept.

### Motto

*"A clear sky over the Zone."*
