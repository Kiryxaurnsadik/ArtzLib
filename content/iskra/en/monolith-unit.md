### Status: DESTROYED

The Monolith psi installation, known in stalker folklore as the "Wish Granter", was destroyed during a joint operation by Iskra, Freedom and Noon in the North, near the town of Novoselki, against an unknown group of IPSF deserters.

### What is known

* The installation was used as a long-term psi anchor for Monolith adherents.
* After its destruction, some Monolith fighters were recorded slipping out of control.
* According to the "Diskette" (AI-O/1), it was during the destruction of the installation that subject O-1 ("Glimmer") received a critical psi impact and lost contact with the control system.

> The detailed operation report has not been published.
