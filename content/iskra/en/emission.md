The phenomenon known as the Emission remains one of the most destructive and least studied processes in the CAZ. It covers the entire territory of the anomalous Zone and is accompanied by a sharp rise in anomalous energy, psi radiation and background radiation. Despite its outward similarity to extreme atmospheric and seismic events, the data taken together point to a multi-field nature that includes physical, gravitational and information components.

This study is based on data obtained through automated observation, analysis of the consequences for living organisms, and the accounts of stalkers and Varta fighters who survived the initial phases of the Emission.

### Initial phase of the anomalous storm
A reliable biological indicator of an approaching Emission has been recorded 1.5 to 2 minutes before the active phase begins: flocks of crows and other birds living near people leave the area en masse, flying strictly south. It was noted that:

* the crows rise from the north-western sector of the CAZ, and their departure is accompanied by loud, harsh cries that differ from their usual ones and sound like an alarm.
* their behaviour is synchronised, which points to a reaction not to sound or light but to early psi radiation from the storm acting on them.

According to meteorological and spectral sensors:

* The sky turns bright red and red-yellow.
* Dense cloud structures form; argon, neon and krypton appear in the air, along with toxic compounds not typical of the natural environment. The concentration of sulphuric and nitric acids reached dangerous levels. The air temperature rose locally by 30 to 40 degrees within seconds. The source of the particle "flow" was the north-west.
* A sharp rise in air ionisation is recorded, followed by changes in the weather (for example: thunder, light haze, stronger gusts of wind, a sudden change of weather).

### Effect of the Emission on humans (clinical and post-mortem data)
During one Emission, several Varta fighters on the outer perimeter did not manage to take full cover. They were evacuated and placed in a biological and chemical protection area, because:

* a critical weakening of the biofield was observed,
* standard medical measures proved ineffective,
* the patients' condition was deteriorating rapidly (disorientation, loss of speech, seizures).

As their condition could not be stabilised, a decision was taken to carry out a post-mortem study.

1. **Neural structures**

### Conclusions about the anomalous storm

1. The Emission is a spatial, energy and information phenomenon covering the whole Exclusion Zone.
2. Biological organisms (birds, humans) react to an approaching Emission earlier than instruments, which points to the direct action of the psi field.
3. The effect on humans is the destruction of the biofield and neural structure, not only physical damage.
4. Data obtained with the ES-1060 mainframe and the AGAT-5109 computer confirm that the Emission is an ordered process and not a chaotic disaster.

### Conclusion
The Emission should be seen not as a natural disaster but as a phase event within the single system of the anomalous Zone, in which information, energy and matter are interconnected, similar to the noosphere (biosphere) theory of V. I. Vernadsky, É. Le Roy and P. Teilhard de Chardin. Studying the storm opens up prospects for understanding the fundamental principles underlying anomalous phenomena, but unfortunately reliable protection outside protected areas is impossible because of the unique structure of the psi radiation and the surge of strong background radiation. The Emission is a signal that will have to be deciphered one day.
