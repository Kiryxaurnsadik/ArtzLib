### Site

"Raduga-1", known among stalkers as the "Brain Scorcher", is a stationary psi installation in the Radar area, north of Yantar and the Army Warehouses. According to stalker accounts, the installation is housed in an underground laboratory of Project "X" and generates a powerful psi field that blocks the way to Pripyat and the ChNPP. Stalkers caught in its radiation become zombified.

### Chronology

* **before 2012**: the installation runs without interruption; the northern territories are practically inaccessible.
* **2012**: the installation is switched off. Who did it and how is still unknown; all that is known is that afterwards the way to the centre of the Zone opened up.
* **after 2012**: repeated psi surges are recorded in the Radar area. The source has not been confirmed.

### Link to the relays

The Monolith's improvised psi relays are probably designed to receive exactly this kind of primary signal (see the report "Improvised psi relay"). In its experimental "Scorch" mode, the Barrier-M repeller partly reproduces the operating principle of Raduga-1.

### Recommendations

* Work in the Radar area is allowed only with psi protection of PZ-2P level or higher.
* If auditory hallucinations appear, withdraw south immediately.
