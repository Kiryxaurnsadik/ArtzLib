The Noon community is made up of former members of Monolith, a once extremely aggressive and fanatical faction. It is still not known by what mechanism these people managed to escape the influence of the Monolith, a source of psi radiation of anomalously high power that once subjugated a considerable human population in the CAZ.

According to stalkers' oral accounts, the Monolith is not merely a cult object but an active psi source, presumably located near the sarcophagus of the ChNPP's 4th reactor unit. In stalker folklore it is described as a wish granter, but the scientific interpretation leans towards a centralised psi resonator able to rewrite a person's motivational and behavioural patterns.

References to such effects in the Institute's archives are fragmentary and classified; nevertheless, indirect data confirm that the Monolith functioned as a long-term psi anchor rather than a one-off phenomenon, although at present it is not broadcasting the psi code.

### The phenomenon of escaping control

The fact that Noon's members escaped the Monolith's influence is the key scientific mystery. Possible hypotheses include:

* A local psi failure in the structure of the source.
* Gradual burnout of the psi code in certain individuals.
* Superimposed external psi interference (for example, emissions or psi anomalies).
* Individual features of the biofield that allowed partial resistance.

It is important to note that the escape is not complete: residual psi markers indicating a former deep synchronisation with the Monolith are recorded in all examined members of Noon. This also shows in a philosophical and religious emphasis in their speech.

### Social disintegration and attempts at socialisation

Noon is currently attempting to integrate into the stalker community. These efforts, however, keep ending in... failure, for the following reasons:

* Their appearance, location and behaviour resemble those of the Monolith.
* The collective memory and stalker tales of mass killings and other horrors committed by the Monolith.
* A lack of trust in the former adherents, whom stalkers call fanatics, madmen, sleepwalkers and murderers.

Stalkers have many nicknames for them, but for most stalkers the Monolith is not an abstract threat but a personal tragedy, and that breeds lasting hostility towards any of its fragments.

Within the community itself there is marked disintegration:

* Some individuals withdraw into solitude (as hermits), completely severing ties with the other members of the community, though they call one another brothers.
* Some form small groups, which then break up over ideological or religious disagreements.
* Certain members still believe in the Monolith, regarding the current state of affairs as a trial and awaiting its return.

Noon is therefore not a monolithic structure: it is a community of people who have lost their common psi anchor and, because of that, are lost and do not know what to do.

### Psychophysiological features of the community's members
Medical and biological observations have revealed a number of persistent anomalies in Noon's members, for example:

### Memory impairment
Owing to the long-term effect of past psi exposure, and with repeated psi exposure, the following are recorded:

* Lapses in short-term memory.
* Confused memories.
* False memories connected with the Monolith.

This points to damage in the structures of long-term neurofield recording.

### Reaction to emissions and psi radiation
Even when in shelters, Noon's members endure emissions far worse than ordinary stalkers. It has also been noted that near psi fields they show:

* An acute pain reaction; some do not show it and try to ignore it.
* Seizures.
* Temporary disorientation.

Biofield analysis shows large tears and thinned zones through which emissions and psi radiation act almost directly.

### The radiological paradox

Against the background of the vulnerabilities described above, a paradoxical fact has been identified: Noon's members show increased resistance to a high radiation background. It is assumed that long exposure to the Monolith's influence has led to:

* A restructuring of cellular repair mechanisms.
* A change in the biofield's response to ionising radiation.
* Partial adaptation of the body to chronic irradiation.

Nevertheless, the community's members continue to use gas masks and protective gear, knowing that their immunity is not absolute.

### Conclusion

In my opinion as a scientist, the Noon community is a unique example of a post-psi-controlled society: people torn out of total psi influence who have not restored the integrity of their former personality and biofield. They are dangerous not by intent but by legacy, and vulnerable not from weakness but because of the depth of the psi exposure they have endured.

From a scientific point of view, Noon may be the key to uncovering several things in the CAZ:

* The mechanisms of psi subjugation (mind control),
* The possibilities of escaping psi control and destroying the psi code,
* The long-term consequences of interfering with the human biofield and the possibility of restoring it.
