This theory and study is devoted to building a coherent theoretical model of the origin, evolution and systematic classification of the anomalous fauna of the Chernobyl Anomalous Zone. The data gathered indicate that the biological objects of the CAZ were exposed to an unknown energy factor, provisionally designated Anomale Energie, capable of rewriting the basic biological and behavioural patterns of living organisms.

### Introduction
Since the Second Disaster, an Anomalische Biosphäre has been observed on the territory of the CAZ, differing from the Earth's classical ecology.

Anomalous fauna is characterised by:

* radical morphological variability
* unstable genetics
* unnatural behavioural patterns
* the ability to survive in extreme energy conditions

Current observations indicate that anomalous energy has affected not only the genome but also the fundamental regulatory mechanisms of biological systems, as if the very bio-code that determines the behaviour of living organisms had been changed.

### Theoretical model of origin

### Anomalous energy as a factor of biological restructuring

Anomalous energy is a complex field combining:

* electromagnetic disturbances
* an unknown information component
* gravitational anomalies
* psi-wave effects

Observations show that long exposure to this energy leads to a phenomenon that can be described as **Biologische Rekodierung**

In effect, the following takes place:

1. The stability of the genome is disrupted
2. Neuroendocrine regulation is restructured
3. New adaptive traits form
4. Mutations become fixed at population level

This gives the impression that the biosphere itself has undergone external editing.

### The role of the X underground laboratories

Archive data and field observations confirm that some species of anomalous fauna are not the result of natural mutation.

Signs of artificial origin have been found in some organisms, including:

* traces of surgical intervention
* non-standard neural implants
* abnormally synchronised behavioural reactions

It is assumed that the underground complexes with the index **X** carried out experiments in:

* neurobiology
* psychotronic exposure
* controlled mutagenesis
* creating biological carriers of anomalous energy

Some mutants can therefore be regarded as **Versuchsorganismen**, that is, experimental biological objects.

### General classification of anomalous fauna

The following classification scheme is proposed for systematisation.

### I. Mutatio Naturalis

Organisms that arose from direct exposure to anomalous energy without human intervention.

### Characteristic features:

* the basic anatomy of the original species is preserved
* increased survivability
* accelerated metabolism
* high aggression

### Beispiele:

* mutated boars
* altered wolves
* anomalous rodents

These species show the classic path of adaptation to a new environment.

### II. Mutatio Extremis

Organisms that have undergone a radical restructuring of morphology and physiology.

### Features:

* significant deformation of the skeleton and muscles
* hypertrophy of the sense organs
* development of new sensory systems
* resistance to radiation and anomalies

This group shows a transition from evolution to **Postevolutionäre Anpassung**.

### III. Organismus Psi-Sensitivus

Organisms that are able to:

* perceive psi radiation
* generate psi waves
* influence the behaviour of other creatures

This group is probably the most closely linked to the information component of anomalous energy.

### IV. Organismus Experimentalis

The most dangerous and least studied category. Signs of artificial origin:

* abnormally high cognitive activity
* collective behaviour
* elements of directed influence on humans
* signs of laboratory selection

These forms were probably created as part of closed research in the X-index laboratories.

### Ecological role of anomalous fauna

Despite their aggression, mutants form a stable ecosystem, the Anomalisches Ökosystem.

The following are observed:

* food chains
* territorial division
* competition between species
* adaptation to Emissions

This points to the formation of a new biological equilibrium.

### Conclusions

1. Anomalous fauna is a new branch of biological evolution.
2. The main factor of change is anomalous energy, which has an information nature.
3. Some species are of artificial origin and linked to the X-index laboratories.
4. The biosphere of the CAZ shows signs of systemic biological recoding.

The Chernobyl Zone is gradually turning into an autonomous biological environment with its own laws of development.
