### Iskra

The **Iskra Scientific Operations Research Group** is a field research group that combines science with operational work in the most dangerous parts of the CAZ. Unlike stationary research institutions, Iskra goes where Varta does not: to psi installations, to Monolith relays and to the northern swamps.

### Leadership

* **Doctor "Arzt"** (Kirill Burnstein Friedrichov): head of the group's research and development, former associate professor at the Yantar research bunker.

### Historical note

The name Iskra is not new to the Zone. In the early 2010s a research group worked under it and was wiped out by Varta; all that remains of it are fragments of reports and a few data caches. Doctor Arzt's Iskra considers itself the heir of that group and carries on its work.

### Operations

* destruction of the Monolith psi installation (Novoselki) together with Freedom and Noon;
* study of captured psi relays and of the "Heart" artifact;
* contact with the Noon community and acquisition of the AI-O/1 "Diskette".
