During field expeditions and analysis of captured samples of Monolith equipment, a stationary installation provisionally designated a psi relay was studied. The device is designed to amplify directed psi radiation and spread it over considerable distances, creating stable zones of psychotronic influence.

### General operating concept of the psi relay
The psi relay works as an amplifier and distributor of a signal coming from a large psi radiation source (presumably the complex known among stalkers as the "Brain Scorcher", official name "Duga"/"Raduga-1").

The psi relay does not generate a primary signal; it receives, amplifies and retransmits the psi code, spreading it over the surrounding territory. Effective operation requires a network of relays placed on high ground. The higher the installation point, the greater the radius and density of the psi field; the maximum recorded radius is 200 to 400 metres.

### Design of the psi relay installation
The design is semi-industrial and half home-made, yet it shows a high level of engineering.
### Main elements:

* The frame base is a heavy support platform for stabilising and earthing the installation.
* The frame beams, 12 in total, form a cage structure that acts as a guiding psi lattice.
* The inner mechanism is a spiral resonator that creates a resonance chamber.
* The artifact mount is the central fixing unit of the psi resonator.
* The fasteners are 10 bolts and 10 nuts (presumably a germanium-containing alloy with psi-absorbing properties).
* A control panel with functions for adjusting the radius and power of the psi field.
* A unique portable power unit.

### Power system of the psi relay
The control panel is powered by a unique power unit containing an unstable, powerful energy element. It is highly likely that the battery contains an artifact from an electrical anomaly. Attempts to open it are considered extremely dangerous:

* a high probability of an energy discharge;
* unstable electromagnetic fields;
* a risk of uncontrolled detonation.

For this reason the power unit is considered a non-serviceable module.

### The role of the "Heart" artifact

The key element of the relay is the "Heart" artifact. Its features:

* It is safe in its passive state.
* When an electric current is applied, it acts as a psi receiver.
* It works as a signal converter:
  * it receives psi radiation,
  * amplifies it,
  * and passes it to the installation's resonator.

In effect, the artifact serves as a bio-psychic antenna and resonance module.

### Network architecture of the relays
A single installation gives a limited radius of roughly 200 to 400 metres. For full effect, a cluster of relays is built:

* the installations are placed within direct signal range of each other;
* a continuous field is formed;
* a single "psi circuit" (Barrier) is created.

Such a network greatly strengthens the effect on stalkers:

* suppression of will,
* suggestion,
* disorientation,
* reinforcement of religious and psychological attitudes.

### Controlling the psi relay
The control panel on the installation allows you to:

* adjust the radiation power;
* change the range;
* switch the installation on and off;
* run basic system diagnostics.

### Protective mechanisms of the psi relay
The installation has an anomalous protective field generated by the active artifact. This has the following consequences:

* it cannot be destroyed from a distance;
* bullets and fragments are deflected;
* blast waves are distorted.

Practical ways to neutralise a relay:

1. Approach to the minimum distance and switch it off by hand.
2. Use several kilograms of explosives right next to it.

### Conclusions of the psi relay study
The psi relay is a key element of the psychotronic control infrastructure. The combination of engineering solutions and anomalous technologies makes the installation extremely dangerous and effective.

The main threat lies not in a single installation but in a deployed network capable of creating vast zones of suppressed consciousness and control over people.
