### Overview

Freedom is an anarchic stalker faction that stands for free access to the Zone and its resources. Its members believe that the knowledge and artifacts of the Zone should belong to everyone, not to the military, governments or corporations. Freedom's long-standing opponent remains Duty.

### Current state

In recent years Freedom has noticeably weakened: leaders have come and gone, and ideology has given way to trading in artifacts and information. New groups have grown out of its remnants, among them Volya, led by Cap.

### Attitude to science

Freedom readily sells artifacts, samples and route information to research groups, but does not recognise the restrictions placed on the Zone by the IPSF and Varta. For field expeditions it is a valuable, if unreliable, source of data.

### Link to subject O-1

According to the materials of the "Diskette" (AI-O/1), Noon's leader regards Freedom as a useful tool, but a secondary force.
