On 26 April 1986 an accident occurred at the ChNPP, which became the largest technological disaster of the 20th century. For the following decades the territory remained an exclusion zone, of interest mainly to radiobiologists and ecologists.

However, at the beginning of the 21st century the first signs of unknown energy processes were recorded that classical science could not explain. Phenomena such as these were observed:

* local electromagnetic disturbances
* spontaneous spatial distortions
* unexplained spikes in background radiation
* anomalous psychophysiological reactions in people

These events were the forerunners of the Second Disaster.

### The Second Disaster and the birth of the Zone
In 2006 a large-scale energy release of unknown nature took place, designated in scientific documents as the Primärer Anomaler Ausbruch. After it occurred, its consequences were observed in the form of:

* the appearance of spatial anomalies
* the formation of anomalous fauna and flora
* the emergence of artifacts
* the formation of a stable anomalous field

At the same time the CAZ was declared a closed territory and surrounded by a barbed-wire perimeter. In some regions and countries the first large concrete walls were built, where security was increased or replaced with remotely controlled automatic turrets.

### Emergence of the stalker phenomenon

In the years after the disaster a new social structure arose, the **Stalker-Ökonomie**. The following began to infiltrate the territory:

* Illegal explorers
* Mercenaries
* Traders
* Scientists or ecological groups.

The main goal became the extraction of artifacts, objects with unique physical properties.

### The psi factor and laboratory research
It was later established that a network of underground laboratories had been operating deep in the Zone. The main areas of research were:

* Mind control
* Influencing collective thinking
* Interaction with the global information field

As a result of the disaster these experiments got out of control. In effect, the experimental infrastructure was cut off from the control system.

### The first major expeditions to the Centre

Military and scientific groups repeatedly tried to reach the centre of the Zone. Their advance, however, was blocked by:

* psi radiation
* anomalous fields
* increased mutant activity

A special role was played by the facility known as the Brain Scorcher, a powerful psi installation creating a barrier to the northern territories.

### The first Super-Emission
The climax of the early period was an event designated:
### Erster Superausbruch

or the first Super-Emission.
Its characteristics are:

* a global energy pulse
* a restructuring of anomalous fields
* a sharp increase in mutant activity
* the appearance of new types of artifacts

After this event the CAZ finally became an unstable self-regulating system.

### The Caribbean experiment
A project of trans-regional resonance. Once the scale of the phenomenon was understood, a hypothesis arose that the Zone is not a local phenomenon. A secret experiment was launched, unofficially named the **Karibisches Resonanzexperiment.**

Aims of the project:

* to test whether anomalous resonance could be created outside the CAZ
* to reproduce the conditions under which anomalous energy forms
* to investigate the global connectedness of the Earth's information field

The Caribbean region was chosen because of:

* high geomagnetic activity
* stable climatic conditions
* its distance from the major powers

Result: the experiment failed. It ended catastrophically. Outside the CAZ the following were recorded:

* Short-term gravitational disturbances
* Satellite communication failures
* Local mental disorders among the staff
* Unexplained disappearances of equipment

The project was shut down immediately. The official data are classified. The unofficial conclusion of the international scientific community is that the CAZ is not a local phenomenon but a node of a global system. After this experiment the anomalous environment of the CAZ expanded several kilometres beyond the originally measured area.

### Schlussfolgerungen

1. The Chernobyl Zone is the result of an uncontrolled interaction between science and an unknown information field.
2. The first Super-Emission completed the formation of the CAZ as an autonomous anomalous system, creating the Zone we can see today.
3. The Caribbean experiment confirmed the global nature of the phenomenon.
4. Present-day realities show that full control over the processes of the CAZ is impossible.

2014  A period of relative calm

After the events at the start of the decade, many factions rushed towards the Centre of the Zone. The remnants of the religious group Monolith took refuge in Pripyat. Preparations began for a military operation to take full control of the ChNPP.

2015  Operation "Monolith-2"

Varta and a state research institute took up positions in the area of the ChNPP and set up a large research complex named Ozarenie (Insight). Work began on reinforced protective suits, along with new attempts to study the central sites of the CAZ.

2017  Failure of the research complex and the return of the psi threat

The activation of automatic systems led to former members of the religious group Monolith regaining control and restarting the psi installations and relays. The north of the CAZ became inaccessible again. At the same time a mutant research project was launched, which ended with the destruction of the research complex near the ChNPP.

2019  Project "Dome"

An attempt to create a safe area free of anomalies by acting on the global information field ended with the emergence of a space-time anomaly and the death of all personnel of the complex in the northern territories.

2020  The present period

The CAZ remains an unstable system. The north and some other territories are closed off by psi installations and relays. Research by scientific institutes continues, but there is no control over all the processes and many technologies have been lost.
